01 — Background

The Problem Space

Campus life at IIT Roorkee is rich with events, clubs, and activities — yet students consistently miss out due to fragmented information channels.

📢

Event Discovery

Students rely on WhatsApp groups, physical posters, and word-of-mouth to discover upcoming events and workshops — with no reliable single source.

🏛️

Club Engagement

Club activities, membership info, and updates are scattered across different channels with no unified directory for students to explore.

📋

RSVP & Tracking

Registration for events is informal — no centralized confirmation, reminders, or participation history exists for students.

"I missed three great events last semester because I just didn't know about them in time." — Common student feedback at IIT Roorkee
02 — Problem Statement

Core Challenge

Students at IIT Roorkee lack a centralized, structured platform to manage their campus engagement — creating unnecessary friction across all touchpoints.

Missed Opportunities

Students miss events due to poor visibility across WhatsApp groups, posters, and scattered social media channels.

Reduced Engagement

Low club participation from lack of structured discovery — especially for new students who don't know what clubs exist.

No History Tracking

Students can't track their registrations, confirm attendance, or view participation history in one unified view.

Information Overload

Students must juggle multiple channels just to stay informed — creating cognitive overload and decision fatigue.

05 — Research Approach

How We Gathered Insights

Our research focused on rapid, accessible data collection from the campus community to understand real behaviors and pain points.

💬

Peer Interviews

Conducted informal interviews with fellow hostelites and club members to understand how they discover events and what frustrates them most.

📱

WhatsApp Observation

Studied how information flows through WhatsApp groups — noting message overload, missed announcements, and lack of structured access.

👁️

Event Observation

Observed real event attendance patterns, registration confusion, and how students navigate between multiple channels to stay informed.

💡

Key Takeaway: Students want a single place to find everything — not another WhatsApp group.

06 — Key Observations

What We Found

1

Students Miss Events Frequently

Most students reported missing at least 2–3 events per month due to lack of timely notification or visibility across fragmented channels.

2

Information is Scattered

Event details spread across WhatsApp, Instagram, physical posters, and email with no single reliable source of truth for students.

3

Registration Tracking is Difficult

Students have no way to view their registered events, confirm attendance, or get reminders in one place after signing up informally.

4

Club Discovery is Limited

New students often remain unaware of clubs that match their interests, reducing participation and engagement significantly.

11 — Design Decisions

Strategic Choices That Shaped the Product

Every major decision was driven by user research and the goal of reducing friction in campus engagement.

Architecture

Unified Platform vs Multiple Apps

Consolidating events, clubs, and RSVP into one app eliminates context-switching and reduces cognitive load for students navigating campus life.

Navigation

Task-Based Navigation

Features are organized around what users want to do — not around system structure — enabling faster task completion and lower learning curve.

Interaction

One-Tap RSVP

Reducing registration to a single confirmation step, with no forms or back-and-forth, directly addresses the biggest friction point identified in research.

Discovery

Single-Platform Discovery

All club and event information lives in one authoritative source, replacing fragmented WhatsApp and Instagram channels that cause information loss.
